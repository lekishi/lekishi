== Decided: control by place, no borders — 2026-10-21

The owner decided on 2026-10-21 that a territory is shown as the places a
state held: the capitals, commanderies and cities that a cited source records
under its control at a date. No border is drawn. Borders are a later follow-up
(<<borders>>).

Two other options were weighed and not chosen.

Schematic areas we draw::
  Polygons drawn by hand around the held places, labelled schematic. These read
  as borders whatever the label says, and no source on usable terms supports
  a line. OpenHistoricalMap has no Warring States polygons, 譚其驤's atlas is
  in copyright, and historical-basemaps is GPL and too coarse for a ten-year
  episode.
Records only::
  No territory kind yet; the annexations stay `changed_boundaries`
  relationships. That is honest, but the map shows nothing of control, which
  RFD 1 asks for.

Why control by place: the sources name exactly this. 史記 and 資治通鑑 record
邯鄲 taken, 薊 taken, 大梁 flooded and Wei's land made commanderies, and 會稽
set up. They name no line. A held place is a cited claim the atlas can draw
without inventing a boundary. The year-by-year table in
link:QIN_UNIFICATION_FEASIBILITY.adoc[the six states' feasibility note] is the
first content.

The owner accepted the shape below on 2026-10-22, and it is built: the kind
amends contract v0 in place, because no published content depends on v0 yet
(the owner's rule of 2026-10-22, in link:CONTRACTS.adoc[the contract record]),
and the six states chapter publishes fifteen snapshots.

== Decided: the territory record — 2026-10-22

A territory record is a *dated control snapshot*: one controller, one date,
and the places a source records it holding then. It is a record kind of its
own, `historical/territories/<id>.yaml`, compiled to the v0 kind `territory`.

[source,yaml]
----
# historical/territories/qin/qin-held-225.yaml (abridged)
summary: "The places the sources record Qin holding after 王賁 flooded 大梁 and took all Wei's land in 225 BCE."
controller: qin-state
date:
  value: "225 BCE"
  mode: duration
  calendar: chinese_lunisolar
  as_written: "二十二年（盡取其地）"
places:
  - place: xianyang
    role: seat
  - place: yingchuan
    gained: han-annexation-yingchuan
  - place: handan
    gained: handan-fall-228
  - place: ji-yan
    gained: ji-fall-226
  - place: daliang
    gained: daliang-flood-225
cite:
  - says: "The annals record 王賁 flooding 大梁, the king of Wei asking to surrender, and Qin taking all his land in 二十二年 (225 BCE)."
    source: shiji
    at: "卷六 秦始皇本紀 二十二年（引河溝灌大梁）"
    quote: "王賁攻魏，引河溝灌大梁，大梁城壞，其王請降，盡取其地。"
----

It compiles to v0 content with `controller_id`, `time`, and `places`, each
place a `place_id`, a `role`, and an optional `gained_record_id` and `note`.

*The record.*

* `controller` is an actor: a state, a dynasty, or a polity. Religious presence
  and cultural influence are not control and never use this kind (RFD 2,
  "routes and areas are published with their interpretation").
* `date` is the window the cited sources support, in the usual date grammar.
  It must be bounded: an open-ended snapshot is an error, because "from then
  on" is an inference no source states.
* *The window* runs from the change its sources date to the year before the
  next change they date, when they record no loss in between; a state's first
  snapshot opens with the chapter's window. In the year a place changes hands,
  the gaining controller's snapshot holds it and the losing controller's ends
  the year before, so year-precision windows never make a transfer look like a
  dispute.
* `places` lists what the controller held in that window. Each entry is a
  place id, or an object with these fields:
** `role`: `seat` for a capital or administrative seat, or `held` (the
   default).
** `gained`: the event or relationship id recording how the controller came to
   hold the place. It lets the card say "taken in 228 BCE" without a second
   claim.
** `note` and `cite`, as on other references. Without its own `cite`, an entry
   is supported by the snapshot's `cite`.
* `cite` supports the snapshot as a whole: the controller, the window, and
  every entry without its own cite. `dispute` works as it does on other kinds.
* A snapshot lists only places a source records held. It is never "everything
  Qin held", and the card says so.

*What is drawn.*

* With the territory orb on, each snapshot whose window holds the playhead
  year is drawn. Each listed place with an asserted coordinate, or with a
  `display_anchor`, gets a disc in the controller's tint under its marker
  (the `control-*` tokens), a ringed disc for a `seat`, split for a disputed
  holding. Nothing is filled, and no hull or line joins the places. The
  legend names each drawn controller beside its tint with the snapshot's
  years, and a place's card lists every snapshot that holds it.
* A listed place with `unknown` geometry is named on the card and not drawn.
  東陽 and 淮南 are the cases.
* Outside every snapshot's window, the atlas shows no control. It never
  carries a snapshot forward and never interpolates between two snapshots
  (RFD 1). A gap between snapshots is a gap in the evidence, and the card
  says so.
* Two controllers may hold one place in overlapping windows only when one of
  the snapshots carries a `dispute`. The map then shows both, marked
  disputed.

*Chapters.* A chapter publishes a snapshot by listing it in the manifest's
`include`. Every listed place, and every `gained` record, must also be reached
by the chapter. A snapshot reached by an approved chapter is in that chapter's
digest, like any other record.

*Validation* (each pinned by a vector in `historical/v0/vectors/territories.json`):

. A controller that is not an actor, or a place or `gained` id that does not
  exist or is of the wrong kind (`reference_kind_mismatch`,
  `unresolved_reference`, `territory_gained_kind_mismatch`).
. No places (`schema_violation`), or one place listed twice in a snapshot
  (`territory_place_repeated`).
. An unbounded `date` (`territory_time_unbounded`).
. A `gained` record that begins after the snapshot's window ends
  (`territory_gained_after_window`).
. One place held by two controllers in overlapping windows with no `dispute`
  on either snapshot (`territory_overlap_without_dispute`).
. A published snapshot with no cited claim (`published_record_without_citation`).

== First content: the six states, 230–221 BCE

The six states chapter publishes fifteen snapshots, under
`historical/territories/qin/`, listed in its manifest's `include`. The places
are the chapter's own records, plus 壽春, Chu's last seat, added for them.

[cols="1,3"]
|===

|Qin
|230–229 (咸陽 as seat, 潁川), 228–227 (adds 邯鄲 and 東陽), 226 (adds 薊),
225 (adds 大梁), 224–223 (adds 陳 and 平輿), 222 (adds 遼東, 代 and 會稽),
221 (adds 臨淄)

|Zhao
|229 (邯鄲 as seat)

|Dai

|Yan
|229–227 (薊 as seat); 226–223 (遼東 as seat)

|Wei
|229–226 (大梁 as seat)

|Chu
|229–225 (壽春 as seat, 陳, 平輿); 224–223 (壽春 as seat, 淮南)

|Qi
|229–222 (臨淄 as seat)

Two departures from the proposal: 上谷 is not a Dai holding, because the
sources place a joint camp there and a camp is not a holding; and each losing
state's window ends the year before the gaining state's begins, by the
transfer-year rule above. The 224–223 snapshot of Chu carries the dispute over
the year of the king's capture, as the event `chu-king-captured` does; Qin's
224–223 snapshot does not list 壽春, because no source names where the king
was taken.

== Borders: a later follow-up

[[borders]]
Drawn borders are deferred, not refused. An `area:` field holding a GeoJSON
file, with its `basis` and cite, can be added to the snapshot later without
changing what a held place means. It needs a source for the line on usable
terms. Candidates to look for: a public-domain atlas plate (for example a
Qing or early Republican historical atlas), a CC0 or CC BY dataset, or an
attributed scholarly reconstruction that we redraw and cite as interpretation.
Until then, no polygon ships.
