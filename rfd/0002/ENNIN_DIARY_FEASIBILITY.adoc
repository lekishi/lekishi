== Purpose

The owner chose on 2026-10-21 to add 圓仁's journey of 838–847 to the Tang
chapter as a further story, and asked for as much content as possible to test
the data and the engine with. This note records what the diary supports, what
was authored, what is missing, and what the engine has to handle that the 753
voyage does not.

== The source

*入唐求法巡禮行記* (Nittō guhō junrei kōki), 圓仁's own diary in four 卷, from
承和五年六月十三日 (838) to 會昌七年十二月十四日 (847). It is first-person and
written day by day, so it is the best-dated itinerary of the period, but it is
a single witness: it is evidence of what 圓仁 saw, heard and was told. Reports
he only heard, such as the date of the 會昌 emperor's death, are marked as such
on their records.

* *Text read.* The Wikisource transcription, which reproduces the 大日本佛教全書
  遊方傳叢書 edition of 1915 (高楠順次郎, based on the 東寺觀智院 manuscript with
  the 池田本 for gaps). Wikisource marks it PD-old; the page text is also
  CC BY-SA 4.0, so records quote the work and cite the edition, as with the
  other Tang sources. Registered as `historical/sources/nitto-guho-junrei-gyoki.yaml`
  and in link:../../docs/SOURCE_REGISTER.adoc[the source register].
* *Excluded.* The transcription's bracketed collation notes, and the modern
  afterword by 梁容若 appended to 卷四, which is not public domain on the
  page's own evidence. Neither is quoted.
* *Locator re-check.* Before release, against 小野勝年『入唐求法巡禮行記の研究』
  (1964–69), which is copyrighted and is consulted, not quoted. Reischauer's
  English translation (1955) is in the same position.
* *Known transcription slips.* 泗州 is written 西州 once and 貝州 appears as
  唐州; the 847 anchorage at 牢山 is an unencodable glyph, so that stop was left
  out. The records quote only passages checked against the text.

Every quotation in the new records (182 of them) was checked by script against
the transcription before it was committed.

== Dates

* The diary dates by the Tang lunisolar calendar, and the records use its
  month and day in the short notation, as the 753 voyage does. The 838 entries
  before the landfall are dated by 承和 and recorded as `regnal`; the diary
  itself notes that 七月二日 was the same day in both calendars.
* 閏三月 of 847 cannot be written in the notation. The three events in that
  month carry a window from the third to the fourth month and keep 閏三月 in
  `as_written`. This is a real gap in the notation, not a guess.
* The diary keeps writing 會昌七年 after recording the change to 大中元年; the
  records keep its wording.

== Places

Display anchors follow the existing practice: a modern city or a Wikidata
historical-prefecture item with a coordinate, never an asserted historical
site. 晉州 is anchored at 臨汾 because Wikidata's own description of the
prefecture says so.

Thirteen places have no sourced identification and are recorded as
`unknown`: 有救嶋, 鎮州, 汾州, 河中府, 同州, 汴州, 密州, the three Silla islands
of 847, and 鹿嶋, 橘浦 and 能舉嶋 in Japan. Wikidata has items for most of the
Tang prefectures but no coordinate on them. Closing this needs a gazetteer the
register allows (the TGN or OpenHistoricalMap) or cited scholarship; CHGIS
would answer most of them and is not adopted.

== What the engine has to handle

This story is a harder test than the 753 voyage, which is its value:

* *Long journeys.* The 840 pilgrimage has 14 stops and the 847 return 14.
* *Breaks in the line.* Unlocated stops in the middle of journeys (鎮州, 汾州,
  河中府, 同州 in 840; the whole Silla leg in 847) exercise the rule that an
  unlocated stop breaks the line on both sides.
* *Repeated places.* A party that stays (海州 in 839: three stops in five
  days) and a place visited twice in one journey (海陵縣 at the landfall and
  again on the canal).
* *Window-dated stops.* Two 閏三月 stops start the 847 return; PR #26's order
  check can only order what v0 orders, so these test that limit.
* *Mixed modes.* Sea, river (canal and 淮) and land legs in one journey, and a
  driven leg (the 838 wreck drifting ashore), each with its own cite.
* *Events without a place,* such as the wreck and the reported death of the
  emperor, which must show in the list without a stop.
* *A story far outside the chapter window* (838–847 against 750–754), which
  the multi-story proposal has to give its own window.

== Open for the owner

* Whether 圓仁's story is one story or is split (crossing and 赤山; pilgrimage;
  長安 and the persecution; expulsion and return). A manifest of 102 steps fits
  the 200-step limit, but four shorter stories may read better.
* Whether to adopt a gazetteer to place the 13 unlocated places, or publish
  them unlocated.
* The 長安 years are covered by selected events only; the diary has much more
  (lectures, festivals, the 回鶻 war, the court) if the story should grow.
