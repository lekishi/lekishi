= RFD 2 source register

== Purpose and rule

This register lists the data, text, image, map, and runtime sources the atlas
may draw on, with each source's terms as read on the date given and the
status the owner's rule gives it. link:../rfd/0002/README.adoc[RFD 2] owns the rule and
the content semantics; link:../rfd/0001/README.adoc[RFD 1] draws what the sources
support; link:../rfd/0003/README.adoc[RFD 3] ships the base geography and runtime
assets; link:../rfd/0004/README.adoc[RFD 4]'s release criterion RC5 checks that
every source in the artifact carries its attribution and terms.

The rule, decided by the owner on 2026-10-10: *a source is adopted by default
only when its terms allow commercial use with attribution*: public domain,
CC0, CC BY, ODC-By, or government standard terms compatible with CC BY. A
source with a non-commercial, share-alike, or research-only term, or whose
terms the project has not read, is not adopted by default. Adopting one
needs a separate owner decision recorded here, and anything taken from it
must stay removable, which is why every coordinate, identification,
quotation, and image records its dataset of origin.

Quoting a public-domain text is quoting the work, whatever the terms of the
transcription it was read in: the record cites the work and the edition,
notes the transcription, and re-checks the locator against a critical
edition before release.

== How to read the status column

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Status |Meaning

|✓ allowed
|Terms read, commercial use with attribution allowed. Adopt; record the attribution line on each record that uses it.

|⚠ conditional
|Allowed within a stated condition: per item or per file, render-only, cite-as-interpretation, or an attribution line that must appear. The condition is in the Notes column.

|? unread
|Terms not yet read on the source's own page, or known only from memory or from a secondary report. Not allowed until read; reading it is a checklist item.

|✗ not allowed
|Terms read; a non-commercial, share-alike, research-only, or no-redistribution term. Not adopted. A permission path, where one exists, is in the Notes column.
|===

Verification dates are the day the terms were read on the source's own page
or an official statement. "Reported" means a secondary report; it does not
count as read.

== Adding or changing a source

. Read the terms on the source's own page; do not rely on a report or on
  memory. Record the date.
. Give the source a row in the group it belongs to, with the status the rule
  gives it and the attribution line the terms require.
. A `?` row is not a licence to use the source; reading the terms is the
  checklist item that turns it into `✓`, `⚠`, or `✗`.
. Every record that uses the source names it as its dataset or edition of
  origin, so a change of status can be acted on.
. A change to a `✗` source's status is an owner decision recorded here with
  its date and the condition accepted.
