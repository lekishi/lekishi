LEKISHI · SYSTEM SHEET

The tokens, type, primitives and page frame proposed for every Lekishi surface, derived from the approved Tang hero. This page is the review copy; once approved it becomes the bar for the chrome, the way the hero is the bar for the map.

Event names, dates and sources are sample content taken from the hero mock. The scene in the frame is a deliberately abstract placeholder, not geography.

Layout

One frame for every scale

Navigation on the left, the selection on the right, time along the bottom, the date across the top. The camera moves from globe to diorama without a cut, so the chrome never rearranges itself at a zoom threshold. Switch widths to see how the regions collapse.

Tokens · colour

One material, one accent

Lacquer for every surface, ink for text, gold only for what is current: the selection, the playhead, the primary action and focus. Contrast is measured against lacquer. Two of the hero's values are raised to pass WCAG AA.

Tokens · evidence grammar

The mark on the map is the badge in the card

RFD 1's evidence states each get one hue and one shape or pattern, shared by the globe and the interface. Possible, disputed and outside are states that modify any mark, not colours of their own.

attested site

Solid ring at the site, the name in its own script.

identified locality

Soft halo whose size is the stated precision.

schematic journey

Long dash through the dated stops.

overland, dated stops

Dotted ink for the land legs.

reconstructed route

Dash-dot with its citation beside it.

dated snapshot

Hatched edge and a date label. No interpolation.

possible at the playhead

Hollow, broken outline, 55 % opacity.

disputed account

Split marker and the word, never the hue alone.

outside the window

Dimmed and struck, with the reason in words.

transmission

Solid arc: learning, religion, texts.

diplomacy

Double arc: embassy, alliance, agreement.

trade

Beaded arc. Conflict uses chevrons, institutions a dashed plum arc.

Owner decisions · 2026-10-13

Decided, and what is left

Everything on this page now reflects these answers. Signing off this sheet is the last step before building starts.

  1. The hero's lacquer, ink and gold, and its three type families, are the foundation.With the two AA corrections shown above.
  2. One frame: navigation left, selection right, time bottom, date top.The region mock's story ribbon across the top is dropped.
  3. Fonts are self-hosted and subset to the glyphs the chapters use.The CJK payload is measured against the three-second phone budget before it ships.
  4. The /design reference is built in development and on pull-request previews only.Each PR's preview shows its design changes; lekishi.pages.dev never carries the page.
