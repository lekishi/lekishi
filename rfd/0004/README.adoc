= RFD 4 Public release charter and roadmap

== Revision note

Revised on 2026-10-10 on the owner's instruction. The earlier charter planned
the work as waves and gates (feasibility, contracts, foundation, fixtures,
baseline, treatment comparison, loop, hardening, launch) and eight acceptance
criteria, all about verification and none about whether a reader learns or
enjoys anything. Gates 1 and 2 were passed and the result was an atlas the
owner is not happy with. This text replaces that plan with a roadmap whose
every step lands something a reader can see. link:GATE1_DECISION.adoc[The
Gate 1 decision record] stays readable and its mission and coverage decision
stands.

== Goal

A publicly accessible atlas in which a reader can follow the 752–754 embassy
and 鑒眞's crossing on a map worth looking at, with the sources one click
away, and then explore the network of missions around it. The site is already
public; what the charter now gates is the announcement, which waits until the
Tang chapter renders its hero.

== Principle

Show, then qualify (link:../0001/README.adoc[RFD 1]). Draw what the sources
support, qualify what is reconstructed in the visual grammar, and keep
editorial status to one line in an About sheet. Content and experience are the
deliverables; process exists to serve them and is sized for one editor
(link:../0002/README.adoc[RFD 2]).

== Roadmap

Three tracks in parallel. Each has an exit a reader sees. The owning
checklists hold the items; this charter holds the order and the exits.

=== Track 0 — Decide and unblock (one week)

. The Tang hero mockup, approved by the owner (RFD 1). Done 2026-10-10: the diorama in `rfd/0001/hero/`.
. PR #14 closed by decision; the treatment comparison retired (RFD 1, RFD 3). Done 2026-10-10.
. The compact authoring format designed and the per-chapter approval
  defined (RFD 2).
. Gazetteer rights decided: CHGIS and CBDB (RFD 2).
. The two-day renderer spike run on the hero, stylized Cesium against a custom Three.js scene, and the renderer decided (RFD 3).

Exit: five recorded decisions.

=== Track 1 — The map worth looking at (four to six weeks)

Basemap, labels, places first, journey lines, arcs, control snapshots, the
timeline strip, the chrome diet, nearby and connected lists, layers, reduced
motion (RFD 1 checklist; RFD 3 for the rendering and the basemap pipeline).

Exit: the served Tang chapter beside its hero, judged by the owner.

=== Track 2 — The data worth exploring (continuous from week one)

Site coordinates for every anchor that has one, the 753 voyage and the 754
overland leg as schematic links, the first cleared images, the embassy
network, 鑒眞's crossings, 圓仁's diary, the Qin control snapshots, the
chapters migrated to the authoring format (RFD 2 checklist).

Exit: a journey can be followed end to end with its sources; the Tang chapter
holds 200 or more events; the Qin chapter has a shape.

=== Track 3 — Announce and learn

After Track 1's exit and the release criteria below: announce the Tang
chapter publicly, record what readers do and ask, and choose the next chapter
from that.

== Release criteria

Verified on the deployed build on the agreed devices before the announcement.

=== RC1 The hero renders

The served Tang chapter matches its approved hero on desktop and on a physical
iPad: the diorama's terrain, symbols, vessel, and chrome at region scale, the
painted relief on the globe, collision-free labels, the voyage's schematic
line, the transmission arc, the timeline, and an image in the card.

=== RC2 A journey can be followed

A reader can select the 753 return voyage, step or play through its dated
landfalls on the map and the timeline, open each landfall's card, and reach
each citation's locator and quoted text within two clicks. The same holds for
鑒眞's crossing to 東大寺.

=== RC3 Everything drawn is traceable

Every marker, line, polygon, arc, and image on the chapter traces to a cited
claim with the interpretation it carries (attested, identified, schematic,
reconstructed, snapshot, depiction) visible in the card. No position, route,
or boundary is invented. Disputed accounts are marked and their alternatives
shown. Undated events are reachable outside time-filtered results.

=== RC4 It is fast enough to explore

Interactive within three seconds on a mid-range phone; sixty frames per second
during pan and playback on the desktop and the iPad; payload per chapter
recorded. Keyboard-only exploration reaches search, timeline, selection,
details, and story navigation; reduced motion is respected; cues do not
depend on colour alone; the event list works without the map.

=== RC5 Rights

Sources, datasets, basemap data, and media carry their attribution and
recorded terms; nothing is reproduced without suitable rights; later
depictions are labelled. Unchanged in substance from the earlier AC7.

=== RC6 Operations

CI green on the released commit (format, type check, tests, build, browser
suite); a documented deploy with a rollback exercised once; a health path and
error visibility the owner can read. Unchanged in substance from the earlier
AC8, minus the Phoenix-era health endpoint.

== Non-goals

* A database or PostGIS before a demonstrated need.
* Native applications before the native checkpoint in RFD 3.
* Editorial tooling or contributor permissions before a second editor.
* Offline maps before a rights review.
* A production stack decision: the stack remains an experiment promoted by
  the measured result.

== Owner decisions

The five in Track 0, plus: a custom domain, an error-reporting provider, and
any paid provider, each separately authorized. The announcement itself is the
owner's call once RC1–RC6 hold.
