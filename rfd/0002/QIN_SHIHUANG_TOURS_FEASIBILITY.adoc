is built from. The chapter is a draft. Only the owner approves it.

== Finding

史記 卷六 秦始皇本紀 narrates all five tours in order, with the places of each
stage. It is the existing `shiji` source, and citing it does not change that
record, so the approved Han chapter stays approved. All 61 quotations were
checked against the Wikisource transcription, which writes 瑯邪 for 琅邪; the
records keep the transcription's form.

The chapter has 22 events, 25 new places and 4 new actors (胡亥, 趙高, 徐巿 and
盧生). It reuses the six-states records `xianyang`, `jingxing`, `shangjun`,
`li-si`, `wang-ben`, `meng-tian` and `qin-king-zheng` without edits.

* 22 places are display anchors pinned to Wikidata revisions: 泰山, 鄒嶧山,
  成山, 之罘, the 琅琊臺 site, 彭城, 湘山 (君山島), 南郡 (江陵), 武關, 博狼沙
  (原陽), 上黨 (長治), 碣石 (昌黎), 九疑山, 錢唐 (杭州), 會稽山, 吳 (蘇州),
  平原津, 沙丘 (廣宗), 九原 (包頭) and the reused ones.
* 雞頭山, 回中, 梁父, 雲夢, 丹陽 and 江乘 have no position. Wikidata gives no
  coordinate for 梁父, and the modern 丹陽市 is not the Qin 丹陽, so it is not
  used.
* 碣石's identification is disputed in modern work. The anchor at 昌黎 asserts
  none.

== Dates

Qin years began in the tenth month, so the last tour's 三十七年十月癸丑 fell
late in 211 BCE. The day is not converted without a Qin calendar table, so the
record gives the window November to December 211. The death on 七月丙寅 is 10 September 210 BCE, the only 丙寅 day
in August and September of that year. The events between 雲夢 and 沙丘,
the illness at 平原津 included, share one window, from December 211 to
September 210: the annals give their order but no month, so the window ends
with the month of the death rather than before it.

== Journey sketches

`qin-shihuang-eastern-tour-219.yaml` and `qin-shihuang-last-tour-211.yaml` in
`historical/journeys/` give two tours in the proposed v1 shape (PR #26),
with `by: river` legs where the annals say 浮江. The last tour tests a journey
that outlives its leader: the cortege carries the body back to 咸陽.

== What the chapter is good for

The chapter is the densest in places: 30 places in 22 events, most of them
anchored. It is a good test for map clutter along the eastern coast, where
之罘 and 瑯邪 each recur on three tours.
