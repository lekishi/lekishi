Diorama symbols for the Lekishi globe

akimeya.json places the symbolic trees and houses drawn around 秋妻屋浦
(lks:place:akimeya) when the camera is low over it. It is the renderer spike's
spikes/renderer/globe/shared/diorama.json, generated by make-diorama.py beside
it from the region-scale spike's placement rules (elevation band, slope limit,
noise threshold; houses seeded around the named places), reformatted one row
per line and otherwise unchanged.

The symbols are symbolic, not to scale, and not a reconstruction: they stand
for kinds of cover and settlement, never for recorded trees, houses or
excavated sites. Ground heights are modern open elevation (see
/globe/ATTRIBUTION.txt).
