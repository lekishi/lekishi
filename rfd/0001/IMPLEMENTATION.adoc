== Phase 1 — Complete exploration loop

- [ ] Add search, layer controls, event details, sources, and separate nearby/connected lists; provide chapter/region/period coverage guidance and verify distinct uncovered, filtered-empty, and unestablished-coverage states without implying historical inactivity or comprehensive coverage.
  - Search, event details, and sources are implemented and browser-checked (link:../0003/CHAPTER_READER_ROUTE.adoc[the chapter reader-route decision]): search covers each chapter's published event text and the name forms its places and participants publish, in local script and in the records' own romanizations; the details panel states the place with its placement note, the occurrence with precision and mode, the record's summary, its participants, its dispute, and its citations with locators and quoted passages; and the sources surface lists the chapter's sources with their recorded attribution and reuse terms. Layer controls, the separate nearby/connected lists, and the coverage guidance remain open, so the item stays unchecked.
- [x] Implement scrubbing, chronological event stepping, playback, and optional camera following; verify playback advances a playhead within a fixed exploration window and stops at its end, distinguishes contextual events from current activity without inventing certainty, and does not mark selections outside-time merely as the playhead moves; provide separate curated "Next in story" navigation with explicit time jumps, without implying a known chronological order for uncertain dates.
  - The playhead is scrubbed with the range input and stepped to event boundaries; playback advances at a fixed rate and stops at the window's end; following is opt-in and a manual pan or zoom stops it without pausing playback (all pinned in `state.test.ts`, `playback.test.ts`, and the browser suite). The story navigator lists the chapter's published story steps in the story's own order, and a step that is a published event is an explicit jump that selects the event and moves the window and playhead to its time (`story.test.ts`, `jumpToEvent` in `state.ts`, browser-checked). An uncertain occurrence keeps its whole span when it is jumped to, and the story's step order is presentation order, not a claim of causality or of a known chronology for uncertain dates.
- [ ] Explain and confirm navigation to related events outside the selected time window; preserve excluded selections in the detail panel with an outside-time notice and explicit return-to-time action, without retaining their map markers or misclassifying uncertain possible matches.
  - The excluded-selection behavior is implemented and browser-checked, and search now reaches events the window excludes: a result outside the window says so on its row, and selecting it keeps the details with the outside-time notice and the explicit return action and without a map marker. What stays open is navigation to *related* events: the atlas has no connections surface yet, so a reader cannot follow a relationship from an event (link:../0003/CHAPTER_READER_ROUTE.adoc[the chapter reader-route decision], "Limitations recorded").
- [ ] Connect a relationship timeline to map selection without assigning religion to territory.
- [ ] Make uncertainty, schematic motion, and supported boundary snapshots visible.
  - Partly done: the details surface states each record's date precision, occurrence mode, placement kind, and why an event has no position; the notice counts unplaced and unlocated records; disputed accounts are marked in the list and explained with their accounts and locators in the panel; and a display anchor states the record's own note that it asserts no historical coordinate. Schematic motion and boundary snapshots remain open (the domain draws points and refuses routes and areas).
