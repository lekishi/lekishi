# The edicts of Aśoka, 261–232 BCE

The manifest is [`ashoka-edicts-261-232.yaml`](ashoka-edicts-261-232.yaml),
a **draft** with no approval block. Dev and pull-request previews serve it, and
production builds do not. What the sources support, the editorial choices, and the
follow-ups are in
[`rfd/0002/ASHOKA_EDICTS_FEASIBILITY.adoc`](../../rfd/0002/ASHOKA_EDICTS_FEASIBILITY.adoc).

## Coverage model

- **Window 261–232 BCE**, from the conquest of Kalinga to Aśoka's death.
- **Story span 301–232 BCE.** Three opening steps (Dionysios' embassy, the
  Taxila legend, the succession and consecration) set the scene before the
  window.
- **Step order is presentation order**, not a claim that one event caused
  another. Relationships sit between events as steps where they explain one.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- Every name, date and position carries a cited claim, and each quotation was
  checked against the Wikisource transcription it was taken from.
- **Regnal years are the source dates.** The BCE windows are Smith's
  conversions (consecration as year 1 in 269 BCE), cited as his interpretation.
- **Five disputes** are recorded and not merged: the succession massacre, the
  meaning of _sambodhi_, the council's date, the length of the reign, and the
  Alexander of Rock Edict XIII.
- **Legend is labelled.** Events from the Ceylon chronicles and the
  Aśokāvadāna say so and cite Smith's retelling.
- **No route is drawn.** Moved pillars are placed where they stand today.
- **Not covered:** inscriptions found after 1920, Chandragupta, the Kuṇāla and
  Tiṣyarakṣitā legends, and everything after Daśaratha's accession.
