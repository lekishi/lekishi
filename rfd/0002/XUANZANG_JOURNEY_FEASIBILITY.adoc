This note goes with the draft chapter
link:../../historical/chapters/xuanzang-journey-629-645.yaml[`xuanzang-journey-629-645`],
candidate 3 of the 2026-10-21 chapter selection. It says what the sources
support, where the chapter had to make editorial choices, and what would raise
its quality before the owner approves it. The chapter is a draft: it has no
approval block, and dev and pull-request previews serve it.

== Verdict

Feasible, and a good test chapter. The sources are public domain and narrate
every stop, so the chapter has 98 events in one traveller's order across about
seventy places from 秦州 to 于闐. What it does not have is a calendar: after
the departure from 長安 in 629, no source gives a month or a year until the
return to 長安 in the first month of 645; even the memorial from 于闐 carries no
date of its own. That gap is the reason it is worth having. It tests the
engine on long runs of undated, overlapping windows, on many stops without a
cited position, and on a journey kind that has to break its line where
positions are missing.

All three texts are public domain. They are quoted from the Wikisource
transcription, which is CC BY-SA, so the records cite the work and its 卷 and
never the page, as the register's Wikisource row requires. CBETA and SAT carry
the same texts with better proofreading, but their terms rule them out as data
(see the register), so the quotations have to be re-checked against a printed
critical edition before content freeze. Every quotation in the records was
checked mechanically against the downloaded transcription. A substring check
cannot tell a sentence from a stray mark, so nine 西域記 name cites that had
been reduced to a lone 。 were found in review and replaced with passages from
the relevant sections.

== Chronology

The biography is not consistent with itself.

* It dates the departure 貞觀三年秋八月, while 玄奘's own memorial from
  于闐 in 卷五 says 貞觀三年四月, and the 舊唐書 says only 貞觀初. The
  departure event publishes 629-08 and records the dispute. The 627 departure
  that modern scholars often argue for has no cited source here, so it is
  not in the records.
* The stays it states (two years at 迦濕彌羅, fourteen months at 至那僕底, five
  years at 那爛陀, two years at 缽伐多 and two with 勝軍, and so on) add up to
  more than the sixteen years between the fixed ends.

So every stop from 高昌 to 于闐 carries an editorial window reckoned from the
stays, with a note that says so. There are eight windows (629..630 out to
迦畢試, through 644..645 for the desert), and they overlap on purpose. Step
order is the biography's narrative order, which the manifest's notes say is
presentation order. Only seven events carry a source date: the departure in 629,
the four steps of the return in 645, the 西域記 in 646 and the death in 664.
The first stops in the northwest (秦州, 蘭州, 涼州) sit in a 629 window.

Cyclic days (景子 for 丙子, 己亥, 己巳) are kept in `as_written`. The records
convert them only to the month, because a day-level conversion needs a cited
calendar table.

== Disputes

* *Departure*: 八月 against 四月 against 貞觀初 (`xuanzang-departure-629`).
* *Death*: the biography's 麟德元年二月五日 (664) against the 舊唐書's
  顯慶六年 (661) at 56 (`xuanzang-death-664`).
* *Origin*: the 舊唐書's 洛州偃師 is noted on the actor.
* *Which khagan*: the biography's 葉護可汗 at 素葉 is not identified with
  統葉護 in the records. Wikidata ends 統葉護's reign in 628, which would
  conflict with a 629 departure, so the identification needs a cited source.

== Places

Of the 77 places, 55 carry a Wikidata display anchor. That is a label
position, not an asserted historical coordinate, and its notes say the
identification is the conventional modern reading. The other 22 carry an
`unknown` position, for one of three reasons:

* The site has no agreed identification, for example 劫比羅伐窣堵
  (Tilaurakot or Piprahwa), 缽伐多, 至那僕底 and 秣底補羅.
* The obvious Wikidata item is a different place. The Han 玉門關 west of
  敦煌 is not the Tang pass north of 瓜州.
* Wikidata rate-limited this environment (HTTP 429) before the lookup could be
  made, for example 迦畢試, 那揭羅喝 and 迦摩縷波.

The 長安 and 洛陽 records are shared and belong to the 圓仁 chapter. With them
the chapter shows 79 places, 57 anchored, and reaches five sources: the three
above, Wikidata, and 圓仁's diary, which the shared places cite.

== Fit with the journey kind (PR #26)

PR #26 made journeys a record kind of their own; the record's shape is still
proposed, and the compiler does not read `historical/journeys/` until the
owner accepts it. The chapter's movement is drafted in that shape as three
journeys in `historical/journeys/xuanzang/`, so it is ready when the kind is
built:

* `xuanzang-outbound-629`: 長安 to 那爛陀, 48 stops.
* `xuanzang-india-636`: from 那爛陀 around India to 缽羅耶伽, 20 stops.
* `xuanzang-return-643`: 缽羅耶伽 to 長安, 19 stops.

Every stop is an event of this chapter with exactly one `site`, `start` or
`end` place, and each journey's order is cited to the biography's narrative.
No stop is strictly earlier than the one before it under v0's partial
ordering, though the overlapping windows mean the check proves little here.
Three events are not stops, because their only place is context: the night
crossing below 玉門關, which he saw and bypassed; the 波羅奢 forest robbery,
east of 奢羯羅; and the loss in the 信度河, mid-river, before the stay at
烏鐸迦漢荼.
No leg states `by`: the biography rarely says how a stage was travelled, and
whether `by` may be inferred is still open for the owner. The journeys name
only 玄奘 as a traveller, because his companions changed from stage to stage.

`xuanzang-travel` stays as the relationship the chapter's step names. Under
PR #26's rule, legs are drawn only between places located at
approximate_point or better, so today the journeys would draw no legs at all:
display anchors break the line.

== Follow-ups, in order of value

. Cite public-domain identifications to raise positions to
  approximate_point. Cunningham's _Ancient Geography of India_ (1871) and
  Watters's _On Yuan Chwang's Travels in India_ (1904–05) are both public
  domain and identify most of the Indian and Central Asian stops.
. Retry the Wikidata lookups for the places listed above, once the rate limit
  clears.
. Add the journeys to the chapter's `include` once the compiler reads the
  journey kind, and add `by` where the biography states how a stage went.
. Re-check the quotations against a critical edition, such as the 中華書局
  edition, which is consulted only, under the register's rule.
. Cite a calendar table for day-level dates of the cyclic days.
. Cite a modern study for the 葉護可汗 identification and for the 627 departure
  thesis, so that both can enter as alternative claims.
