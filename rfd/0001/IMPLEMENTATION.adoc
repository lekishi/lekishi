= RFD 0001 implementation checklist

Implements link:README.adoc[RFD 1: Historical exploration and visual interactions].
All work below is proposed; the concept images are not implementation evidence.

== Phase 0 — Scope and interaction proof

- [ ] Bound the Japanese missions to Tang China chapter to one source-checked mission and selected learning/adaptation links with RFD 2; define small contrasting test examples rather than additional launch chapters.
- [ ] Select representative desktop/laptop and physical iPad-class tablet hardware and browsers within RFD 3's primary device scope; define phone responsive-layout checks without requiring full exploration parity.
- [ ] In later separate implementation threads, compare geographic/symbolic and illustrated local-detail treatments for both a Tang mission and a bounded Qin-conquest episode, preserving time and selection through globe-to-local navigation.
- [ ] Validate distinct visual forms for campaigns, trade, journeys, and divisions.

== Phase 1 — Complete exploration loop

- [ ] Add search, layer controls, event details, sources, and separate nearby/connected lists; provide chapter/region/period coverage guidance and verify distinct uncovered, filtered-empty, and unestablished-coverage states without implying historical inactivity or comprehensive coverage.
- [ ] Implement scrubbing, chronological event stepping, playback, and optional camera following; verify playback advances a playhead within a fixed exploration window and stops at its end, distinguishes contextual events from current activity without inventing certainty, and does not mark selections outside-time merely as the playhead moves; provide separate curated "Next in story" navigation with explicit time jumps, without implying a known chronological order for uncertain dates.
- [ ] Explain and confirm navigation to related events outside the selected time window; preserve excluded selections in the detail panel with an outside-time notice and explicit return-to-time action, without retaining their map markers or misclassifying uncertain possible matches.
- [ ] Connect a relationship timeline to map selection without assigning religion to territory.
- [ ] Make uncertainty, schematic motion, and supported boundary snapshots visible.

== Acceptance evidence

- [ ] Inspect rendered globe, region, selected-event, and connection states on desktop and touch layouts; verify manual pan/zoom stops camera following without pausing time, following stays off until explicitly resumed at the current playback position, and programmatic camera movement does not cancel following.
- [ ] Verify keyboard access, reduced motion, non-color cues, and a readable event list.
- [ ] Demonstrate a nonmilitary story is as navigable as a campaign; verify simultaneous story layers share one map, time window, and playhead, identify memberships without color alone, preserve shared events without duplication, and imply no unsupported cross-story relationship.
- [ ] Confirm labels and scene geometry against reviewed data, not generated mockups.
- [ ] Record measured performance and remaining limitations with RFD 3.
