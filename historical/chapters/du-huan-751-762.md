# 杜環 and the 經行記, 750–762

> **Draft.** The manifest [`du-huan-751-762.yaml`](du-huan-751-762.yaml) carries no
> approval. Development and pull-request previews serve it under the draft
> banner. Production leaves it out of the public site, and only a signed-in
> member of the `lekishi` organization can open it there, as a server-only draft
> (RFD 6). Only the owner approves it, with `pnpm approvals`.

This note is the chapter's feasibility record: what the sources can carry, what
the chapter publishes, and what it leaves out and why. It was written on
2026-10-21, when the chapter was drafted as candidate 5 of the chapter-selection
pass, to give the atlas a second Tang chapter that reaches west of the Pamirs
and a test bed for journeys whose stops cannot be drawn.

## Feasibility in one paragraph

Feasible as a chapter about **a war, a lost book and a decade of contact**, not
as a drawn journey. 杜環's 經行記 is lost; about 1,500 characters survive as
quotations in 杜佑's 通典 (卷一九一–一九三), with one sentence of biography. That
sentence dates his departure (天寶十載, 751, "to 西海") and his return (寶應初, 762,
by merchant ship through 廣州), and nothing in between. The fragments describe
lands, not a route, and 通典 quotes them in its own order. Around that core, the
Tang histories give a dense, dated frame: the 750 石國 raid, the 751 defeat at
怛邏斯, and 大食's envoys, troops and raid on 廣州 in 756–758. So the chapter can
carry 23 events with real dates and disputes, but 杜環 has only two stops of his
own, both dated, and the far one (西海) cannot be placed.

## Sources and rights

The four Chinese sources are public-domain works read in Wikisource
transcriptions, under the register's Wikisource row: quote the work, cite the
edition, note the transcription, re-check against a critical edition before
release. 通典 has its own register row because it is the only witness of 經行記.

Two public-domain sources were added on 2026-10-22, each with its own register
row, and both read in Internet Archive scans:

- **Ibn al-Athīr's al-Kāmil** (Tornberg's edition, vol. 5, 1871), for the Arabic
  account of 750–751 (owner's decision, 2026-10-22). The scan's OCR is unusable,
  so the year-133 notice (p. 344) was read from the page image. Records give it
  in English and quote no Arabic, because the site has no Arabic face yet; names
  are transliterated.
- **Hirth and Rockhill's Chau Ju-kua** (1911), for the modern identifications
  of 經行記's names (note 14 to its 大秦 chapter, pp. 108–110).

資治通鑑, 舊唐書 and 新唐書 are **one source per work** (owner's decision,
2026-10-22). The chapter first drafted juan-scoped sources beside them so the
approved Qin and Tang chapters stayed untouched; they were folded into
`zizhi-tongjian`, `jiu-tangshu` and `xin-tangshu`, whose titles now name the
work and whose cites name the 卷 in their locators. That changes the digests of
the Qin and Tang chapters, which need re-approval.

Wikidata items were read on 2026-10-21 through `Special:EntityData`, and each
coordinate cites the item revision current that day. The Wikidata and Wikipedia
APIs were rate-limited from the cloud environment.

## How the chapter handles what the sources do not say

- **杜環 is never placed at the battle.** 杜佑 says he "followed 高仙芝's western
  campaign and reached 西海 in 天寶十載". That he was captured at 怛邏斯 is the
  usual modern reading, recorded in notes and not asserted.
- **經行記's observations are undated windows** (751..762) on the places they
  describe: the Chinese craftsmen and the weekly sermon at 亞俱羅, and the 東道使 at
  末祿. A fragment that only describes a land (拔汗那, 康國, 拂菻, 苫國, 摩鄰,
  師子國, 波斯) is not an event; those lands, except 波斯, which has no place
  record, are context places on the event of writing the book.
- **An identification is placed only where it is cited.** Hirth and Rockhill
  (1911, pp. 108–110) read 亞俱羅 as Kufa, from its Syriac name Akula, and 苫 as
  Sham (al-Shām, Syria). 亞俱羅 is an `approximate_point` at Kufa (precision
  locality) and 苫國 one at the Syria region's coordinate (precision region),
  each with a claim attributed to them. The same note reads 拂菻 as Syria, not
  Constantinople; 拂菻 keeps its label anchor at Constantinople, whose notes now
  record the disagreement. 末祿 = Merv and 拔汗那 = the Ferghana valley remain
  uncited `display_anchor` positions. 摩鄰, 西海, 白石嶺 and 相州 carry
  `unknown` geometry.
- **What was searched for the rest** (2026-10-22): Chavannes's Documents sur
  les Tou-kiue (1903, p. 297) renders 西海 as "la mer d'occident" without
  placing it; Hirth's China and the Roman Orient (1885) translates the 摩鄰
  fragment without identifying it; Bretschneider's Mediaeval Researches (1888)
  has nothing on 末祿. 張一純's 經行記箋注, the standard commentary, is in
  copyright and was not reachable.
- **Chinese accounts of 大食 are kept as Chinese accounts.** 白衣 and 黑衣 大食
  are separate dynasty actors beside the state 大食, and 阿蒲恭拂 keeps the
  sources' name, with al-Manṣūr only as a Wikidata external id. The change of
  house is an undated succession: both histories tell it as an alternative
  account (一云, 或曰) without a year, so the chapter neither dates it nor borrows
  the Arabic histories' 750.
- **Not asserted:** that the 亞俱羅 craftsmen were prisoners of 751, that
  paper-making came west with them, that 杜環 stopped at 師子國, or that the 751
  war caused the later alliance.

## Journeys

The owner decided on 2026-10-21 (PR #26) that a journey is a record kind of its
own, `historical/journeys/<id>.yaml`; the record's shape and drawing rules are
still proposed in [the authoring format record](../../rfd/0002/AUTHORING_FORMAT.adoc),
and nothing of it is built. The chapter writes no journey record until it is.
Each 杜環 stop is an event with exactly one `start`, `end` or `site` place, and
everything else is `context`, so the proposed shape can use them unchanged. The
journey the sources support is:

```yaml
# historical/journeys/du-huan-west-751-762.yaml (not written: the kind is not built)
travellers:
  - actor: du-huan
    role: participant
stops:
  - du-huan-reaches-xihai-751      # 西海: unknown geometry
  - event: du-huan-return-762      # 廣州: display anchor
    by: sea                        # 因賈商船舶
```

摩鄰 is not a stop. 通典 introduces that fragment with 又云 ("it also says"), so
it describes a land and does not say 杜環 went there.

Under the proposed drawing rules this journey draws **nothing**: 西海 is unknown
and 廣州 a display anchor, so its one leg is not drawn. It is a case for the open
question "is a journey whose stops are all unlocated worth publishing for its
list and card alone?", which the proposal answers yes, and so does this note.

An approximate area would let 西海 be drawn without pretending to a point. The
data side exists already: an `approximate_point` at `region` precision draws as
a wide halo, as 苫國 now does. 西海 cannot take one yet, because no source
identifies it: Chavannes leaves it as "the western sea", and no open source
read here places it. The engine side would be to draw
`source_supported_area` polygons, which the atlas refuses today, and to let a
journey leg end at an area or a region-precision point, drawn as a softer line
than a leg between located stops. A second journey, 李嗣業's
retreat (`talas` start, `baishi-ling` site), would test a leg from a located to
an unlocated place.

## Steps outside the window

Three story steps fall wholly outside the 750–762 window: the 744 marriage of
和義公主, the 748 destruction of 碎葉 and the 801 presentation of 通典. The atlas
projection requires every such step's caption to start "Coda — ", so all three
carry it, including the two that come before the window. The engine has no
label for a prelude, so a reader sees 744 and 748 badged as "coda". This
chapter is a test case for fixing that in the engine rather than in the data:
the projection already knows each step's dates and the window, so it could
badge a step "before" or "after" from them and stop requiring the caption
prefix.

## Exclusions

The Arabic accounts of 751 other than Ibn al-Athīr's, and the Arabic side of
the Abbasid revolution; the founding of Baghdad (762) and the move from Kufa; paper-making at
Samarkand; the 747 小勃律 campaign; the 安祿山 rebellion except notices naming
高仙芝, 李嗣業 or 大食; the captive 突騎施 khagan, 吐蕃 chief and 朅師 king of 751 as
records; 經行記's purely descriptive passages as events; 涼州, 鄯州, 鳳翔, 靈武 and
淮南 as places.

## Decisions and what is open

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-22:

1. **One source per work** for 資治通鑑, 舊唐書 and 新唐書: done. The Qin and
   Tang chapters' digests changed and need re-approval. Other chapters keep the
   same split pattern (`zizhi-tongjian-qin-ji-2`, `shiji-six-states`,
   `dushi-fangyu-jiyao-six-states`, `shiji-han-frontiers`) and could be folded
   the same way.
2. **Cite modern scholarship** for the 經行記 names: done where an open source
   exists (亞俱羅, 苫國, and the 拂菻 disagreement). 末祿, 摩鄰 and 西海 stay
   uncited.
3. **Ibn al-Athīr as a competing account** of 750–751: done.

Still open:

1. Publish 杜環's journey for its list and card although it draws nothing? This
   note recommends yes, once the journey kind is built.
2. Draw approximate areas, and let journey legs end at them (see Journeys)?
3. Derive a "before" or "after" badge from the window, instead of the "Coda — "
   caption (see Steps outside the window)?
4. An Arabic face for the site, so Arabic names and quotations can be shown in
   their own script.
