The end of the state of Han

233–226 BCE

In 233 BCE the king of Han offered his submission to Qin; by 230 BCE Qin had taken the state, and in 226 BCE its former capital 新鄭 revolted. Follow 韓非's embassy and disputed death and the cession of 南陽.
