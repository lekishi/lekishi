Darwin said "draft it" on 2026-11-04 to the IA reset proposed after [[ux-direction-2026-10-23]]. Delivered, docs only, no product code:

Four tabs built from the 39 served chapters' real records: the world at a moment (canvas globe + folded era strip, chapters alive at a year, threads via shared places), chapters as vertical time (10 culture columns, region/kind filters, cross-column links), the Tang embassy as a 38-step guided tour on painted relief, Roma across 6 chapters as one timeline. - RFD record: `rfd/0005/IA_RESET.adoc` + README note + IMPLEMENTATION section, draft PR #68 (https://github.com/lekishi/lekishi/pull/68), branch `claude/project-thread-s2n64u` on top of main c7318b4.

**Data finding:** the audit's claim that each chapter copies its places was wrong for the records. World-first layout already shares ids: 88 places and 41 actors referenced by 2+ chapters, 91 chapter pairs linked, 683 places, 930 events, 1065 story steps (main at 27f5ac3). Only the search index and per-chapter projection re-key them. Painted relief basemaps (hypsometric + hillshade) come in seconds from the cached Terrarium tiles (`.cache/globe/terrarium/4` world, `/7` region) with numpy + Pillow.

**Choices put to Darwin (unanswered when written):** (1) world page replaces home, or home keeps a short list beside it; (2) schedule the Civ scene requirements (terrain LOD, units on journeys, settlements, figures) with the IA pages or after; (3) the region/culture grouping per chapter (the mock's 10 columns are hand-assigned; a `region` field on the manifest is proposed).

**How to apply:** before building the world page, chapters page, place/people pages or tour mode, read PR #68's review and Darwin's answers; judge any screen against "does it show correlation and relationships" and "is this boring".
