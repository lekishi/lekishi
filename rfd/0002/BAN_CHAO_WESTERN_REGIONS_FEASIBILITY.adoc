== Finding

後漢書 卷四十七 (班超's biography) gives a dated narrative from the 蒲類海
campaign of 73 to his return to 洛陽 in 102. 卷八十八 西域傳, which says it
draws on 班勇's report, gives the kingdoms one by one and 甘英's mission to
大秦 in 97. Together they carry 17 dated events. All quotations were checked
against the Wikisource transcription. The 卷85 and 卷88 transcriptions mix in
simplified forms; quotes are taken only from passages in traditional forms.

== What the chapter holds

17 events, 2 relationships (the protectorate and the 月氏's tribute), 13
places (9 label anchors, the Han–Wei city of 洛陽 as an approximate point, 3
with no position) and 12 actors.

== Shared ids

The oasis kingdoms reuse the ids of the 玄奘 PR's records (`khotan`,
`kashgar`, `kucha`, `agni`, `baluka`, `yiwu`) with the same Wikidata pins.
Whichever PR merges second folds its names and cites into the existing file.
`agni` (焉耆) has no position in either PR.

== Placement and disputes

* 條支 and 大秦 have no position. 甘英's western sea is not identified, and
  `gan-ying-mission-97` draws no line past 安息, whose anchor sits at
  Wikidata's coordinate for Hecatompylos.
* `shule-coup-74`: the biography dates 班超's seizure of 兜題 to 74, while the
  西域傳 dates it to the winter of 永平十六年 (73). Both are cited, and the
  record does not choose.

== Dates

Most events are year-precision. The 莎車 campaign is a two-year window
(87–88). The return to 洛陽 is 永元十四年八月, given as the window August to
September 102.

== What the chapter is good for

The chapter is a long-span biography with a single far-flung journey, and it
overlaps the 玄奘 chapter's places six centuries apart. It tests place records
shared across chapters and eras.
