== Finding

The embassy has two literary notices, both in 後漢書: the annals (卷一下
光武帝紀下) enter "東夷倭奴國主遣使奉獻" in the first month of 中元二年, and the
東夷列傳 (卷八十五) adds the envoy's title (大夫) and the gift of a seal and
cord (印綬). They are enough for a short chapter of five steps.

The find of the gold seal on 志賀島 in 1784 is dated and placed from Wikidata
alone (Q2975212, P575 and P189). Those statements carry no reference, and no
contemporary account of the find (the 甚兵衛 口上書) is cited. The
government pages that were reachable (九州地方整備局) give no year. The
heritage databases that would (文化遺産オンライン, 福岡市博物館) refused the
container or had no stable page. A better source for the find is follow-up
work.

== What the chapter holds

5 events (the embassy, the gift, 光武帝's death, the 帥升 embassy of 107 as a
coda, and the 1784 find as a coda), 3 places and 3 actors.

== Placement and dates

* 倭奴國 has no position. The source places it only at the southern edge of 倭.
* `shikanoshima` reuses the 圓仁 PR's id and Wikidata pin. The anchor is the
  island, not the find-spot.
* 光武帝's death on 二月戊戌 is converted to 29 March 57, which was checked as a
  戊戌 day. The embassy's 正月 is the window February to March 57.
* The 1784 find is a Gregorian date, `calendar: gregorian`.

== What the record does not claim

The records never say the seal of 1784 is the seal of 57. That
identification, and the reading of 漢委奴國王, are modern scholarship that no
cited source states.

== What the chapter is good for

The chapter is the smallest chapter and the longest span: 57 to 1784 through
two coda nodes. It tests the timeline's handling of a 1,700-year gap and a
chapter with only two drawable places.
