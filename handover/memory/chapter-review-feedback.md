On 2026-10-21 Darwin pushed five fixes on top of the Aśoka draft (branch claude/project-thread-rgp6mf, commits eca8f3e9..cf48086e):

- **Dates converted from a scholar's chronology carry its stated error.** Smith says his reign dates may be off by up to three years, so each converted BCE year became a window three years either side, citing that bound. The story span widened to match.
- **A scholar's reading stays apart from what the source says.** Rock Edict XIII claims a conquest by the Law in five Greek kings' realms. That envoys carried it to their courts is Smith's reading, cited as his interpretation, not as the edict.
- **Legend is marked in the summary**, which readers see, not only in notes.
- **Moved monuments get display anchors**, not approximate_point at a site: their modern spot is not the event's place.
- He also fixed the atlas so chronological stepping stays inside the selected window when an event's support runs past an edge.

**Why:** the atlas must not show more precision or certainty than the evidence gives.
**How to apply:** when drafting a chapter, widen converted dates by the source's stated error, cite readings as interpretation, put legend in the summary, and anchor anything that was moved as a label. Before pushing on a shared branch, fetch first: Darwin may have pushed to it. See [[ashoka-chapter]], [[authoring-format-decisions-2026-10-18]].
