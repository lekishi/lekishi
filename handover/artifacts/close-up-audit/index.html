Lekishi Close-up Audit

Every chapter place, opened on the deployed preview at phone size and zoomed in step by step, in three runs: main's tiles, the first preview before the land cover (64ec3f0), and the preview with the land cover. Below that, the tiles themselves are cropped at each place and at seven points between places, so the comparison does not depend on where the camera stopped.

Verdict with land cover

What holds now

  • The sand blocks are gone at 蘇州黃泗浦, 揚州 and 難波. Low land is painted from the land cover's fields and water, and sand only lies next to water.
  • Flat land now has real features from ESA WorldCover: rivers, canals, lakes, irrigation ponds, woods and tree lines. This is clearest at 蘇州黃泗浦, 揚州, 難波, 潁川郡, 新鄭 and the Nara basin (平城宮, 東大寺, 唐招提寺).
  • Forest on hills and fields in valleys now follow the land cover instead of only the relief, for example at 多禰島, 大宰府 and 石籬浦.
  • 16 of the 17 places loaded level 10 on the preview in this run. 唐招提寺 stopped at level 9 again, because its wheel zoom ends a little higher; its level 10 tiles exist (see its crop). Levels 0 to 4 and every height tile are byte-identical to before, so the world view did not change.
  • The preview's tiles were downloaded and match the local build byte for byte. CI passed on all eight browser projects after the legend fix.

What still does not hold

  • The North China Plain (新鄭, 潁川郡 and the land between them) is almost all cropland, so it stays one even green with sparse specks. There is no field pattern to show at these levels.
  • There are no towns. Built-up land is painted as fields, because the land cover is from 2021 and today's cities did not exist in these years. The decision card said "fields, rivers and towns"; towns are not there.
  • Rivers, coasts and reservoirs are today's. At 難波 the shore is Osaka's reclaimed shore, not the 8th-century Kawachi lagoon. The globe shows present-day ground, and the About sheet says so.
  • In hills with grassland or small fields (Korea, Aso) level 8 has harsh lime specks. Some lakes near 蘇州黃泗浦 keep a thin pale rim from the beach rule.
  • The trees at 秋妻屋浦 are still the diorama, not the tiles.
The first audit, before the land cover (64ec3f0)

What holds

  • The finer tiles exist and the preview serves them. Main's tiles stop at level 7. On the preview, 16 of the 17 places load level 10 in this run (唐招提寺 stopped at level 9 because its wheel zoom ended a little higher), and level 10 tiles exist around all 17, and level 8 covers the whole chapter area, including the seven points between places.
  • In hills and on coasts the close-up gains real shape: the shoreline at about 100 m and the ridges and valleys. That is 10 of the 17 places and 5 of the 7 points between them.
  • "Zoom out to the whole chapter" returns to the chapter view from every place (the last frame of each strip).

What does not hold

  • I said the ground stays sharp anywhere in a chapter's area. That was overstated. On flat plains the only change is a faint mottle, and it looks almost the same as before: all three Qin chapter places, 蓬萊宮含元殿, the North China Plain and the middle Yangtze.
  • On low deltas and shores, levels 9 and 10 are broken: 蘇州黃泗浦, 揚州 and 難波. A beach rule paints all land 0.5 to 6 m above sea level as sand, and land below 0.5 m as water. The elevation steps in whole metres, so the result is blocks. The rule is older, but it only shows at pixels finer than level 8, so it first appeared with levels 9 and 10.
  • The trees at 秋妻屋浦 are the diorama, 3D models drawn only there. They were there before this work and do not come from the tiles.

How the ground is painted now

Three things are measured data now:

  1. Shape comes from open elevation (the Terrarium tiles): about 1 km samples to level 7, about 250 m at level 8 over the chapter area, about 125 m at levels 9 and 10 near each place.
  2. Land cover comes from ESA WorldCover 2021 (10 m, CC BY 4.0) over 105–141°E and 21–42°N. The builder reads it at about 300 m over the area and about 75 m near the places. Each pixel is painted from the share of each class (forest, shrub, grass, fields, wetland, bare ground, water), relative to the area's average so the land cover's edge leaves no rectangle. It fades in from level 5.
  3. Base colour comes from Natural Earth II, as before.

Still painted by rules: sea colour from depth, rock on steep slopes, a little relief shading, and a fine mottle outside the land cover. Built-up land is painted as fields.

The tiles themselves

Each row is one 0.12° square (about 13 km) cut from the tile images: main's finest tile (level 7), the first preview at the finest level there, then the land-cover preview at levels 8, 9 and 10 where they exist. Pixels are enlarged without smoothing, so you see exactly what the tiles hold. The land-cover preview's 1,347 bundles were downloaded and are byte-identical to the local build.

How this was captured

Headless Chromium with a software WebGL renderer, 390 × 844 at 2× pixel density, one fresh browser context per place, with Playwright's video recording. "Main" is this branch's own build with static/globe replaced by main's builder output (3,162 tiles, finest level 7), served from the container. "First preview" is claude-project-thread-anpeq8.lekishi.pages.dev at 64ec3f0, and "land cover" is the same address at fd09917 and then 3442294 (the deploy changed during the run; both carry the same tiles, and 3442294 only shortens the legend's credits). The app code is otherwise the same, so the tiles are what differ. Wheel zoom is not perfectly repeatable between two runs, so the cameras can stop at slightly different heights; the tile crops above do not have that problem.
