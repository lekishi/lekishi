== Make the map worth looking at

- [ ] Region and local scale as a diorama: 3D terrain from open elevation with exaggeration, slope and elevation tinting, shallow-water shading, symbolic forest and fields scattered by terrain, warm directional light with shadows (RFD 3 owns the renderer and the terrain pipeline).
- [ ] Symbolic settlements at attested sites: glyph buildings of a kind, scaled for legibility, never a reconstruction; a schematic vessel or party at the playhead's stop.
- [ ] Ship a self-made relief basemap (hypsometric tint, shaded relief, coastlines, rivers) as static tiles for the globe and the 2D fallback: zoom 0–4 global, 0–7 over chapter extents, provider-free.
