= RFD 3 atlas frame and scene contract

== Provenance and scope

On 2026-10-14 the owner asked for the new globe to replace the old one with
link:../0005/README.adoc[RFD 5]'s atlas frame, the screens of the navigation
canvas, and the globe extras of link:../0001/README.adoc[RFD 1] (journeys, arcs,
territory, the diorama, the minimap), made to "look like a product", with
RFD 2's data migration left for later. This record covers what that changed in
the browser architecture RFD 3 owns: the renderer boundary, the address, the
search index, the fonts, and what the browser keeps.

No historical content, publication set, ledger row, or contract version
changed. The projection gained fields it reads from the published records
(relationships; each name's romanization and language), nothing more.
link:STATIC_ARCHITECTURE.adoc[The static architecture decision] stands.

== Decision

* *The map draws places, not events (RFD 1, "Places first").* The scene
  carries `places` (one per place with a sourced position, with its window's
  event count and its evidence form: attested site, identified locality, or
  display anchor), and the renderer reports `onSelectPlace(placeId)` instead
  of an event id. The workspace decides that a place with one event opens the
  event. This supersedes the event markers described in
  link:GLOBE_RENDERER.adoc[the globe renderer decision]; that record stays
  readable as written.
* *The scene carries the extras as data.* `journeys` (dated stops, a vessel or
  party at the reached stop), `arcs` (relationship families, drawn on
  selection and by layer), `territories` (dated snapshots; none are published,
  so the layer is offered disabled) and `dioramas` (the 秋妻屋浦 close-up,
  fetched from `/dioramas/akimeya.json` below 220 km) are derived in
  `overlays.ts` from the published relationships and a per-chapter overlay
  table. The renderer draws them; no renderer object enters the scene.
* *The scene safe area is a view input.* The workspace measures how much of
  the globe its regions cover and passes it as `scene.safeArea`; the renderer
  frames flights and follow into the clear zone with `camera.setViewOffset`.
  The renderer also reports the camera's centre and height
  (`onViewChange`) for the locator, and flies home once per `homeToken`.
* *The address carries the reader's position.* `query.ts` reads and writes
  `?event=`, `?place=`, `?step=` and `?from=`/`?to=`; the chapter route writes
  it through SvelteKit's shallow routing (a jump pushes, an in-view change
  replaces) and hands Back and Forward to the workspace. Nothing is written
  for the address the page opened at, so a stale link stays visible until the
  reader moves, and the arrival plate has said what it opened instead.
* *Search is one prerendered file.* `/search-index.json` is built from the
  same projected chapters the pages render and fetched the first time search
  opens; the build invariant requires it to cover every served chapter.
* *Fonts are self-hosted and subset at build time.* RFD 5's faces are pinned in
  `scripts/fonts/sources.json` by commit and sha256, cut by fontTools to the
  characters of the records and the interface (`pnpm sync:fonts`, generated
  into `static/fonts`, never committed), and the build invariant fails if a
  chapter's searchable text uses a glyph the subsets lack. Each face ships with
  its OFL text.
* *The browser keeps one thing.* "Continue where you left off" stores the
  last chapter position per chapter in `localStorage`, guarded so a blocked or
  corrupt store is the same as none, and the switcher says it is kept on this
  device only. No account, server, or third-party request is involved.

== Open

* RFD 2 calendars: the date bar shows the source's own date expression beside
  the year; converted era dates wait on RFD 2's calendar contract.
* A `SceneArc` does not yet say which of its ends is a schematic stand-in (an
  actor drawn at its court); the card states it, the map does not.
* Arcs are bowed sideways a little so an arc never lies on a journey between
  the same two places; the owner reviews that on the preview.
* The chapter list's own search still uses the in-chapter matcher rather than
  the shared index with its scope fixed.
* Frame times of the extras on the reference devices are the owner's, on the
  pull request's preview.
