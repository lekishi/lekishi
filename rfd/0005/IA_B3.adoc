= Four doors: the B3 information architecture
:state: Decided 2026-11-19, except where marked open

The owner chose B3: Home must be "immediately usable for people who already
visited it before". Time, World and Places are three ways across the chapters:
by year, on the board and by place. The <<captures,design captures>> and
https://ampcode.com/threads/T-01a1dddb-ca1e-7684-b67f-d1277e8b6edf[design discussion]
show the layout and behaviour. Use the design tokens and count the served
catalog, not the mock's CSS or its development counts.

This decision supersedes the previous link:IA_RESET.adoc[IA reset]'s
world-first Home, culture-column chapter page and individual place pages.
B3 has no people page and makes no decision about a future one. It supersedes
the search-first Home, globe and time ribbon in link:README.adoc[RFD 5]. The
owner declined directions A and C, a globe-first Home, culture lanes, the tree
of ages, and a hero, in-page search and Places block on Home. B3 does not redraw
the chapter workspace or change the evidence grammar or chapter query contract.

== What each door answers

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Door |Reader's task
|Home `/` |Resume a chapter, or choose a first one. Continue and up to three other recent chapters precede World and Time summaries, then served chapters grouped by manifest region. The prerendered first visit starts with the three served chapters with the most story steps and an About link.
|Time `/time` |Compare chapter bars by region and year in Regions in time, promoted from `/dev/timeline`. `?from=&to=` sets a window. Open World at `?year=` only for a read year the board can show.
|World `/world` |Explore the campaign board, today one turn per year from 750 to 762, promoted from `/dev/world`. `?year=` opens a turn; the board and its existing query controls carry over.
|Places `/places` |Trace a place shared by two or more served chapters. Layout M1 puts the index beside the map on wide screens and below it on tablet and phone. M2 and M3 were declined.
|===

Places groups the index by map position, with each place listed once. A
hex-tile map draws region borders along tile edges. Grouping by chapter label
and drawing straight region lines were declined. Places without a position
are listed in their own group and are not drawn. A place opens at All time.
At a moment—one chapter's telling of a dated event at the place—only that
chapter's reach lights up. The bar steps by event, not by year, without autoplay.

About at `/about` takes the introduction off Home. It gives the tagline,
three ways in, how to read the board, sources and uncertainty, what is kept
on the device and map attribution. The full attribution remains at
`/globe/ATTRIBUTION.txt`. The chapter's own About sheet remains in place.

== Navigation is distinct from workspace controls

Home, Time, Places and About share persistent chrome. From 800 px up, the
logo links Home, followed by Time, World, Places, a search trigger with `/`,
and About. Below 800 px, the logo, Search and About icons stay in the header;
four named icon links sit in a safe-area-aware bottom bar. The current page
uses `aria-current` and gold, with an underline in the wide text links.
The standard 22 px logo keeps the bilingual lockup from 640 px up.

The search trigger and `/` open the existing dialog, not a separate search
route. Places adds one shared-place result per place without removing the
per-chapter results. The active workspaces keep their own controls: until the
open navigation decision below is made, World and chapters do not receive the
new chrome and their logo links Home.

== Addresses carry the view; memory is unchanged

Places selection pushes `/places?place=<id>` and refocuses the map. Stepping
through moments replaces the current entry with `&event=<id>`. A stale place
or event is reported.

Continue and Recently opened read the existing `src/lib/atlas/memory.ts`
store: one position per chapter, its query, short label and timestamp, plus
the most recent chapter's ID. There is no new persistence or server sync.
Blocked or empty storage leaves the first-visit choice. Member cookies are
separate, as described in link:../0006/README.adoc[RFD 6].

== Settled by the design review, for the owner's review

* Home mounts no live scene. Still images are a separate choice below.
* On Time, `?q=` filters the chart by chapter, place or year; it is not a
  second site search. `/` opens the site dialog, and Escape clears the filter.
* In Places, add `&chapter=<id>` only for an event ID shared by several
  moments at that place. Without a matching chapter, open the first in bar
  order. A single-chapter place URL offers a chapter link, not a selected row.
* The phone time bar keeps the chapter name on its own truncated line, and
  map labels appear when zoom permits.

== Open decisions

* *Home stills:* whether Continue and World carry still images.
* *Place regions:* whether to adopt the nine sketched regions and boundaries
  as drawn, and if so whether to store them as records or derive them. Chapter
  manifest regions are unaffected.
* *Workspace navigation:* design review recommends four doors on World above
  its own controls, with the phone dial clear of the bottom bar, and leaving
  chapter chrome as shipped. Alternatively, both workspaces keep their own
  chrome. The mock used shipped workspace captures and does not settle this.
