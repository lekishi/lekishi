The fixture is a preparation snapshot, not release content.
- [ ] Review imported candidates and preserve their external identifiers and licenses; verify historical/local-script labels and local-script/romanized search aliases without conflating successor places, and record the initial editor's approval of the published content revision in the file-based workflow.
  - The approval portion is done for both chapters: the owner's editorial approval of the published Tang revision was recorded on 2026-09-25 and of the corrected Qin revision on 2026-09-27, both in link:../0002/PUBLICATION_APPROVAL.adoc[the publication approval gate]. The search-alias portion is implemented: the projection carries each place's and actor's published name forms, romanizations, and translations as search aliases, and deliberately excludes the contract's modern equivalents, because those are kept out of the historical name list and must not become the string that finds a historical place (`src/lib/atlas/projection.ts`, `search.test.ts`, link:../0003/CHAPTER_READER_ROUTE.adoc[the chapter reader-route decision]). The item stays open for the successor-place checks and the critical-edition re-check of the cited transcriptions, which the ledger rows keep open.
- [x] Represent contradictory accounts and records lacking exact dates or map positions; present an attributed main account with a visible dispute indication and cited alternatives in the detail panel, without merging competing claims or requiring switchable map interpretations.
  - Content: 韓非's death is published as one disputed record with three cited accounts and a dispute summary, and 雲陽, 南陽, and 潁川郡 carry `unknown` geometry rather than invented positions; the Tang chapter publishes its own disputed records the same way. Surface: the shared chapter surface marks a disputed record in the event list and shows the record's published summary with the accounts that challenge it and their locators in the details panel, on both chapters — the badge and the accounts are no longer a review-only presentation (link:../0003/CHAPTER_READER_ROUTE.adoc[the chapter reader-route decision]). An event the atlas cannot place keeps its place name, its reason, and its record's own note instead of a coordinate, and the panel states the date precision and the occurrence mode. Browser-checked on both chapters.
- [ ] Verify every published relationship has a cited evidentiary basis; distinguish attested claims from cited scholarly interpretations, label schematic geometry separately, and exclude original contributor hypotheses from public content.
  - Both chapter suites pin the content side: every published relationship carries cited claims, neither chapter publishes route or schematic geometry, and no published revision carries research notes. The details surface now labels placement kinds and shows each claim's citation with its source, locator, and quotation, but it does not yet state a claim's `basis` (`attested` versus `cited_interpretation`) or open a relationship, so the item stays open.
