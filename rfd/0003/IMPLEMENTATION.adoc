= RFD 0003 implementation checklist

Implements link:README.adoc[RFD 3: Browser architecture and native options], as
revised on 2026-10-10. Completed items keep their evidence; open items are the
rendering and delivery roadmap.

== Done before the revision

- [x] Static SvelteKit build with the Cesium runtime, prerendered routes, the build invariant, and the browser suites against the artifact. Evidence: link:STATIC_ARCHITECTURE.adoc[the static architecture decision], `scripts/check-static-build.mjs`.

- [x] One chapter route serving every chapter through one catalog. Evidence: link:CHAPTER_READER_ROUTE.adoc[the chapter reader route].
- [x] Cloudflare Pages deployment from `main` at `lekishi.pages.dev`, authorized 2026-09-24.

- [x] Renderer spike built and measured: the hero's diorama scene on stylized Cesium, on a custom Three.js scene, and on a Rust/wgpu probe compiled to wasm, one harness. Evidence: link:RENDERER_SPIKE.adoc[the renderer spike record].

== Rendering

- [ ] Domain and scene types for journey lines, relationship arcs, control-snapshot polygons, and places-with-events; the projection accepts the published geometry interpretations.
- [ ] Renderer support for the diorama: terrain mesh with exaggeration, slope and elevation tinting, shallow-water shading, directional light with shadows, instanced symbols scattered by terrain, glyph settlements at sites, a vessel at the playhead's stop, lines, arcs, and polygons, behind the unchanged `AtlasRenderer` boundary.
- [ ] Offline terrain pipeline: open elevation to a shipped mesh or heightmap per chapter extent, with the exaggeration and the source terms recorded.
- [ ] Offline basemap pipeline: open elevation and Natural Earth data to raster tiles, zoom 0–4 global and 0–7 over chapter extents, shipped in the artifact with attribution; payload recorded.
- [ ] Symbol library: conifer, broadleaf, house, hall, pagoda, vessel, party, with terms recorded.
- [ ] Labels projected from the 3D scene with collision handling and CJK coverage.
- [ ] The handheld tier: build-time tile classification from the elevation, a tileset and sprite sheet, a PixiJS renderer behind the `AtlasRenderer` boundary with grid cursor, d-pad and A/B input, and the dialogue box; measured on a mid-range phone.
- [ ] The pixel look for tablet and desktop: quarter-resolution render of the diorama with a reduced palette, switchable on demand.
- [ ] Collision-free labelling with CJK and Latin font coverage.
- [ ] Playhead-driven animation of journey lines without the renderer owning time.
- [ ] Timeline strip component (SVG) driven by workspace state.
