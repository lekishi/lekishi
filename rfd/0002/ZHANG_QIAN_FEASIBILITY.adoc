== Finding

The 大宛列傳 (史記 卷123) is the account of both missions and is enough on
its own for the order of events. It dates almost nothing: the first mission
leaves "in the 建元 years" and returns thirteen years later. The return is
dated to 126 BCE by joining it to the 匈奴列傳's death of 軍臣單于. The 漢書
biography (卷61) is cited as supporting text. The 西南夷列傳 (卷116) dates the
search for a road to 身毒 through 蜀 to 元狩元年.

== What the chapter holds

13 events, 13 places (7 label anchors, one approximate point at the Han city
of 長安, 5 with no position) and 12 actors. The campaigns of 123 and 121 BCE
are shared with the Han–匈奴 wars chapter, so one event belongs to two
stories.

== Placement and dates

* 康居, the 大月氏 court, the 單于庭, 羌中 and 犍為 have no position. 大宛,
  大夏 and 烏孫 are display anchors at the Fergana basin, Balkh and Issyk-Kul.
  `daxia` and `wusun` use the same Wikidata pins as the 玄奘 PR's `balkh` and
  `issyk-kul`.
* Every event of the first mission shares the window 140–127 BCE, because the
  source gives no years in between. v0 cannot order them, so the story order
  is presentation order only.
* The 烏孫 mission's return is a window, 119–115 BCE. The 漢書's 百官公卿表
  (卷19) dates 張騫 as 大行 and would narrow it, but its transcription could
  not be fetched on 2026-10-21.
