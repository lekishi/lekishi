== Purpose

The owner asked for the Japanese missions to Tang to be added to the Tang
chapter as a further story, around the 750–754 embassy, and for as much
content as possible to test the data and the engine with. This note records
what the sources support, what was authored, what is missing, and what the
records ask of the engine.

== What was authored

All records sit under `historical/*/tang-missions/`. No chapter reaches them
yet, so the build compiles none of them and nothing is published.

Every claim carries a quote, and every quote was checked as a verbatim

== Sources and access

* *日本書紀* 卷廿二–廿九 for 623–670, including 伊吉連博德書, the envoy's own
  account of the 659 embassy, which the compilers quote in notes. It is cited
  as quoted there.
* *續日本紀* for 701–781.
* *日本後紀* for 801–806. Only parts of the work survive. 卷十 and 卷十一 on
  Wikisource are reconstructed from quotations (逸文), so some entries have no
  day; 卷十二 holds 藤原葛野麻呂's report of the 804 crossing, the longest
  first-hand account in the set.
* *續日本後紀* for 834–839.
* *舊唐書* 卷一九九上 and *新唐書* 卷二二〇 for the Tang side.

All are public-domain works in Wikisource transcriptions, which the register's
Wikisource row already covers (cite the work and the edition, quote only the
classical text). `docs/SOURCE_REGISTER.adoc` needs no new row.

The existing `jiu-tangshu` source is titled for its 日本國 section; these
records also cite its 倭國 section. Broadening the title to the whole 東夷
chapter fits better, but that source is part of the approved chapter, so it is
left for the next re-approval rather than changed here.

== Conflicts kept

Where two sources disagree, both are recorded and the event carries a visible
dispute. Neither account is preferred.

* `mission-633-gao-biaoren-departure`: 日本書紀 records a ceremonious visit;
  both Tang histories record 高表仁 quarrelling over ritual and leaving
  without delivering his message.
* `mission-659-iwashiki-wreck`: 伊吉博德 says the islanders of 爾加委
  destroyed the ship; 難波吉士男人 says it struck an island and capsized.
* `mission-703-wu-zetian-banquet`: 舊唐書 dates 粟田眞人's embassy to
  長安三年 (703); 新唐書 to 長安元年 (701), the year it was appointed.
* `mission-778-xuanzheng-audience`: the two returning envoys' reports date
  the audience with the emperor to 3/22 and 3/24.
* `mission-806-takashina-request`: 舊唐書 has 高階真人 ask to take the
  students home because they had made progress; 新唐書 has them wishing to
  stay, after "more than twenty years".

== Gaps

* *894.* The cancelled embassy of 菅原道真 is not authored. Its sources,
  日本紀略 and 菅家文草, have no transcription on zh or ja Wikisource
  (checked 2026-10-21). The next step is an NDL Digital Collections scan of a
  public-domain edition, under that register row's per-item condition.
* *653, second ship.* The chronicle's notice of the wreck of 高田根麻呂's
  ship is not in the Wikisource transcription of 卷廿五. The dispatch event
  says so and nothing is invented for it.
* *752.* The 750–754 embassy is the existing story. This set does not repeat
  it; its relationships end where that chapter begins.
* *Embassies that never sailed.* The 762 embassy, cut back after a ship
  split its stern, is recorded (`mission-762-cancelled`). Other appointments
  that came to nothing were not searched for.
* *Damaged characters.* The transcription writes ■ for missing characters.
  Quotes keep them; where a name is damaged (the Tang monk 道■ of 736) the
  record does not name the person.
* *Day precision in 801.* 日本後紀's 逸文 give 延暦廿年 with a day but no
  month for the appointment, so it is recorded at year precision.

== Places

22 of the 24 new places carry a display anchor citing `wikidata` "Q… (P625),
revision N", placed at the modern site, district or city the summary names.
The anchors are for display only and assert no historical coordinate. Where
the modern item covers only part of the old unit (筑紫 at 筑前, 松浦郡 at the
modern city of 松浦) the note says so. Two stay unknown on purpose:

* `nikawe-island` (爾加委): no modern identification is sourced.
* `kunlun` (崑崙國): a general name for the lands of the southern sea; which
  coast 平群廣成 reached is not sourced.

A stop at either of these breaks a journey's line.

== Journey candidates for PR #26

The events are shaped as dated stops, so these can become journeys once the
kind is decided:

* 659, 伊吉博德's ship: 難波 → 筑紫大津 → 越州 → 洛陽, with 石布's ship
  breaking off to 爾加委 and five survivors reaching 括州.
* 702, 粟田眞人: 筑紫 → 楚州塩城縣.
* 734–739, 平群廣成: 蘇州 → 崑崙 → 長安 → 登州 → 渤海 → 出羽. The longest
  single voyage in the set, and the only one through 渤海.
* 777–778, out and back, with the four ships home by four routes (松浦,
  出水, 耽羅, and the broken first ship's halves at 甑嶋 and 天草).
* 804–805, 葛野麻呂: 松浦 → 赤岸鎭 → 福州 → 長安 → 越州 → 明州 → 對馬.

== What the records ask of the engine

* *Unreached records are never checked for references.* A record no manifest
  reaches can point at an id that does not exist, and the build passes. With
  four drafts sharing ids across branches this is easy to hit. A tree-wide
  reference check, separate from compiling, would catch it.
* *One main claim per field.* The publication checks allow one `main` claim
  per pointer, so two claims that each support part of a field (one per
  participant, say) must mark all but one `supporting`. Per-participant
  citation would say this more exactly.
* *Disputes are per event.* An event with a conflicting claim on one field is
  marked disputed as a whole. That is right for these five events, but a
  field-level dispute would let the reader show which part is contested.
* *Court acts.* 28 events are court acts the entry gives no place for; each
  is anchored at the court's seat with the same note. A place role
  for this, or a chapter-level default, would remove the repetition.
* *Unknown places in journeys.* Landfalls with no sourced identification
  (爾加委, 崑崙) break a journey's line. PR #26 should
  say how a journey shows a leg it cannot draw.
