= RFD 4 Gate 1 decision: the Tang launch mission and its coverage model

== Provenance

This record answers link:README.adoc[RFD 4]'s Gate 1: the owner reviewed the
Wave 1 findings, selected the launch mission from
link:../0002/TANG_MISSION_FEASIBILITY.adoc[the Wave 1A feasibility
recommendation], and accepted the delivery approach. It records the decision and
its bounds; it does not rewrite the feasibility report, the contract record, or
the delivery evaluation, which stay readable as the evidence they are.

The decision was relayed for implementation on 2026-09-24 with these settled
working assumptions: select Candidate B as recommended; accept the coverage
model; defer purchases, library-only sources, specialist outreach, and
restricted or non-commercial CHGIS/CBDB data; use only sources and media whose
publication rights are already suitable; and keep this work to reviewed content
rather than deployment, merging, or provider changes.

== Decision

=== The launch mission

The first public chapter is *Candidate B, the 752–754 embassy of 藤原清河
(Fujiwara no Kiyokawa)*, as the feasibility report recommends. The report's
comparison found it the best-corroborated candidate: a court chronicle plus a
near-contemporary voyage biography plus the Tang histories, all public domain and
free to read, with dated geography and public-domain image candidates.

=== The coverage model

Accepted without variation, as the feasibility report states it once and uses
throughout:

* *primary mission window 750–754* — appointment, dispatch, the Tang New Year
  audience, the return voyage, the return reports, the rewards, and the 754
  ordinations;
* *included coda nodes outside that window at 758, 759, 763, and 779*, each
  separately sourced and labelled as a coda node rather than as a mission effect;
* *the outbound voyage of 752 is excluded* — it is undocumented in the sources
  checked, so the chapter carries no content, no route, and no map geometry for
  it.

=== The delivery approach

The delivery approach accepted at Gate 1 is the one already recorded in
link:../0003/STATIC_ARCHITECTURE.adoc[the static architecture decision]: a
statically built SvelteKit site with Cesium, no application server, no database,
and historical content that stays renderer-independent. This record adds no
account, purchase, provider, domain, or deployment authorization.

independent review, or specialist review. RFD 4's AC2 stays open until the owner
records editorial approval for a published revision, and RFD 2's rule that AI
assistance is not editorial approval applies to the fixture as prepared.

== What this record does not decide

* *Owner editorial approval of the fixture.* The fixture is prepared for review;
its publication set records self-review only. AC2 is not satisfied by this
record.
* *Gate 2 and the treatment comparison.* The Qin comparison fixture, the shared
baseline, and the geographic/illustrated treatment comparison remain Wave 2 and
later work.
* *Purchases, library access, specialist outreach, and CHGIS/CBDB use.* Deferred
as stated; the fixture uses no restricted dataset, which is why several places
carry no asserted coordinates.
* *Media rights.* No image or media asset is published. The public-domain
東征伝絵巻 scroll and the 鑒真 statue photograph remain candidates needing
per-file clearance and a "later illustrated copy, not evidence" label.
* *Deployment, provider accounts, domains, or external release.* Unchanged and
still requiring explicit owner authorization.

== Evidence recorded with this decision

* The chapter fixture: `historical/chapters/tang-mission-750-754/` — 14 places,
12 actors, 29 events, 9 relationships, 1 story, and 9 sources, published as one
publication set. Its README records the inventory, the evidence, the uncertainty
handling, the deferrals, and the limitations.
* Validation: `src/lib/historical/contract/tang-chapter.test.ts` validates the
tree with the contract's own semantics, checks the seal and publication rules,
and exercises the chapter's coverage model — the primary window, the coda years
and their labels, the 752 outbound exclusion, the no-route-geometry rule, the
kept textual variants, and adversarial readings that must be rejected.
* Loading boundary: `src/lib/historical/chapter.ts`, so the chapter is loadable
through the existing contract without a second validator and without a renderer
dependency.
* Sealing: `pnpm seal` now reseals the worked examples and this chapter;
`pnpm verify` is the full check.
